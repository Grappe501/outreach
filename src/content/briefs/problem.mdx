## Arkansas campaigns cannot organize effectively with incomplete or outdated voter information.

Many organizations rely heavily on VAN. VAN remains important—but any voter-contact system is only as useful as the data feeding it.

### Common problems

- Missing or outdated phone numbers
- Missing mobile numbers for texting
- Incomplete email information
- Old or incorrect mailing addresses
- Duplicate or disconnected voter records
- Valuable contacts stuck in spreadsheets, phones, notebooks, or separate systems
- Local knowledge that never becomes part of a usable campaign database

We already work from important Arkansas voter-registration information from the Secretary of State, plus records from voter and ballot-initiative efforts.

**That is a usable foundation on Electd.**

For verified emails, mobiles, and enriched addresses at scale, campaigns need an enhanced file—planned via [L2](/data/) and the [cooperative](/cooperative/).
