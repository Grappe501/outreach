## Strengthen records. Don’t abandon tools.

Organizations that rely on VAN can use enhanced information to identify missing contact fields, correct outdated records, and improve communication with voters.

Depending on technical and licensing requirements, data can be prepared for matching, updating, appending, or importing into existing workflows—including VAN and the [Electd](/platform/) platform.

**Campaigns do not have to abandon the tools they already know.**

**They can strengthen them.**

### Feed Electd

Cleaner phones and emails mean better self-serve and managed outreach on Electd. [Platform →](/platform/) · [electd.io](https://www.electd.io/)
