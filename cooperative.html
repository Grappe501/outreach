These amounts are minimums

We negotiated cooperative participation to keep entry costs as low as possible for the widest range of Arkansas campaigns and county organizations. The figures below are the minimum one-time data contributions required for your tier.

If your campaign can afford more, please contribute more. Additional support accelerates acquisition and strengthens the statewide file for every participant.

Metropolitan rule: Local races in metropolitan areas with 50,000 or more residents must pay the county rate ($300 minimum), not the local $200 minimum.

Contribution tiers Minimums

Local races

$200 minimum one-time

Justice of the Peace and other races smaller than a county—except metro areas of 50,000+, which pay the county minimum.

Plus $149/mo if using Electd.

County organizations

$300 minimum one-time

Enhanced information for the county. Also the required minimum for local races in metro areas of 50,000+.

Electd $149/mo is optional for counties that only want data.

State House and State Senate

$500 minimum one-time

Plus $149 per month for campaigns using Electd.

Statewide campaigns

$700 minimum one-time

Plus $149 per month for campaigns using Electd.

How the cooperative model works

If 50 counties contribute at the $300 minimum:

50 × $300 = $15,000

More than 60% of statewide acquisition.

If three statewide campaigns and ten local races contribute at minimum:

3 × $700 = $2,100

10 × $200 = $2,000

Cooperative total so far:

$19,100

Ten legislative campaigns at the $500 minimum complete the purchase:

10 × $500 = $5,000

Total: $24,100

Above-minimum contributions reduce the number of participants required—or expand what the cooperative can deliver.

What participants receive

Access to the portion of enhanced L2 data appropriate for campaign, district, county, local geography, or statewide jurisdiction—subject to final vendor licensing.

  • Improve voter-contact records
  • Update internal databases
  • Strengthen VAN matching
  • Build better call, text, email, and mail lists
  • Apply HaystaqDNA issue & behavior models
  • Reduce wasted communication spend

Founding participation is conditional

Cooperative pricing is available only if enough organizations participate to fund acquisition.

The statewide file will not be purchased—and pricing will not be finalized—unless participants collectively cover the acquisition cost.
