Campaign ownership

Your campaign contacts remain your campaign contacts.

Statewide enhanced data provides a foundation. Each campaign’s organizing work—and private relationships—remain its own.

Every campaign builds information that belongs to that campaign, including:

  • Supporter identifications
  • Volunteer relationships
  • Donor prospects
  • Yard-sign requests
  • Event attendees
  • Personal voter conversations
  • Local leaders
  • Community relationships
  • Notes from doors, calls, texts, and meetings

Participating campaigns can add their own contacts while keeping those records separated from other organizations.

Private voter contacts are not automatically placed into a shared statewide pool.

They can be firewalled and visible only to the campaign or organization that created them.
