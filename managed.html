What’s included in Managed

  • List building and segmentation from your approved universe
  • Message and creative coordination for each send
  • Email, SMS, MMS, and direct-mail send management
  • Compliance and delivery coordination basics
  • Performance reporting after each program
  • Coordination with your VAN or existing workflow where needed

Managed rates reflect both channel cost and the labor to run the program. Sample floor prices below are the low end of market managed ranges; final pricing is set before launch based on volume and scope.
