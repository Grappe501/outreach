Data without the cooperative

Platform subscribers receive SOS registration information plus whatever contacts and voter records the campaign provides. That is enough to organize, communicate, and track work inside the system.

The Data Cooperative is a separate, optional purchase for campaigns that want the professionally enhanced statewide file (verified emails, mobiles, addresses, and more).

Usage is pay-as-you-go

The $125 subscription covers the software and your data workspace. Channel sends are billed at raw/platform sample rates when you run them yourself.
