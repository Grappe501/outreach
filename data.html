Why L2

For over 50 years, L2 has been a trusted source for enhanced voter, consumer, and constituent data. Their national voter file is positioned as accurate, frequently updated, and rich in segments for traditional and digital targeting.

L2 aggregates many data sources into an ecosystem for selecting, analyzing, and micro-targeting the voters you need to reach—faster. Access is web-based; no desktop software install is required.

Using L2 data is straightforward

L2 describes a four-step workflow for turning hundreds of millions of records into usable lists:

  1. Define your universe Choose filters across demographic, voter, consumer, and modeled behaviors.
  2. Pull counts and analyze Compare criteria across districts and view selections geographically.
  3. Solidify your selection Use mapping to cut turf and confirm counts.
  4. Export your records Download contact lists (Excel, CSV, or DBF) and engage.

Arkansas cooperative participants receive the portion of enhanced data appropriate to their jurisdiction, subject to the final vendor licensing agreement—and can use it to improve VAN, Electd, or internal databases.

What Arkansas campaigns receive

We are working to acquire a professionally enhanced Arkansas voter and consumer file (~1.6 million records) through L2, funded by the cooperative—not by any single campaign alone.

Where available, enhanced fields typically include verified emails, mobile/text-capable numbers, telephone numbers, current mailing addresses, registration and district information, and additional contact attributes useful for outreach.

  • Improve voter-contact records and reduce wasted calls/texts/mail
  • Strengthen matching into VAN or other organizing systems
  • Feed cleaner universes into Electd for self-serve or managed programs
  • Keep campaign-owned contacts private—see private contacts

Without cooperative purchase: campaigns still use Electd with SOS registration data plus records they provide. L2 enhanced data is the optional upgrade.
