L2 · HaystaqDNA

160+ preference models. Scores from 0 to 100.

The L2 HaystaqDNA models identify voters by likely feelings toward issues and candidates—and by behaviors that matter to campaign contact strategy. Available in L2 DataMapping and across digital platforms and integrations.

How campaigns use the models

Each model is presented as a score from 0–100, with flags to identify those most likely to support, oppose, or behave in a defined way. That lets serious campaigns move beyond party registration alone—into issue intensity, channel responsiveness, and turnout propensity.

Behavior

  • Activism, charity, church attendance
  • Email / SMS / live-call responsiveness
  • Political donations, ticket splitters
  • Ballot drop-off, community connection

Economy

  • Affordability, housing, inflation fault
  • Unions, capitalism, income inequality
  • Gig work, tax cuts, infrastructure
  • Crypto, economic anxiety

Healthcare & education

  • Abortion, ACA, Medicaid expansion
  • Medicare for All, vaccines
  • School choice, charter schools, funding
  • Teachers union, CRT books

Ideology & partisanship

  • Fiscal / social / general ideology
  • Democrat type, Republican type
  • Primary election ideology
  • Third-party partisanship

Environment & government

  • Climate, Green New Deal, gas tax

  • Regulations, public transit

Media & consumer

Military, borders & foreign policy

Turnout

Candidates & officials
